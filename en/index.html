MoneyLeft

Always know how much you have left, until your next payday.

What it does

Most budgeting apps tell you what you have already spent. MoneyLeft tells you the other number: what is still yours to spend once the rent, the instalments and the subscriptions have been taken out — between today and your next payday.

It runs on your iPhone. There is no bank connection, no advertising and no tracking. An account is optional, and only exists so you can find the same data on another device.

Before you look for it in the App Store

MoneyLeft is currently published on the Italian App Store only, and its interface is in Italian.

These pages are in English so that the privacy policy and the terms of service can be read in full before installing anything — and so that support is reachable in English today.
