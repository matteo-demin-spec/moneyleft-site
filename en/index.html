FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No, and you could not even if you wanted to. MoneyLeft does not connect to any bank and asks for no banking credentials: you enter the transactions yourself, or import them from a CSV file. That is a choice, not a technical limit.

Do I have to create an account to use it?

No. The free plan works without signing up, and the first-run guide does not ask you to. An account is for finding the same data on more than one device and for using MoneyLeft Pro, which is registered to your account.

Where does my data end up?

It stays on your device, in a file protected by the encryption of iOS. It only leaves the phone if you turn syncing on yourself, or when you export a backup wherever you decide. We sell data to nobody and there is no advertising tracking.

Can I export what I have entered?

Yes, and it stays free for ever even without Pro. You will find export and full backup in Settings, under "Your data". A backup can be restored on another device.

What happens if the subscription ends?

Nothing to your data: it all stays where it is. Only the Pro features switch off, including automatic syncing. Whatever you had already created beyond the free plan's limits is not deleted.

Which iPhone do I need? Does it run on Mac and Apple Vision Pro?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later. If you change phone you get everything back: by signing in again with an account, or by restoring an exported backup.

MoneyLeft is an iPhone app, and as such it also downloads onto Macs with Apple silicon (from macOS 14) and onto Apple Vision Pro (from visionOS 1): the interface stays the iPhone one, in a window.

To be straight with you: on Mac and on Vision Pro the app is available but I have not tried it myself, because I have neither of them. On iPhone I do, with every version. If you use it on a Mac or a Vision Pro and something does not add up, write to me: that is the quickest way to get it fixed.

Does the month really start from my payday?

Yes, and it is the whole point of the app. You set the day you get paid and every calculation — how much you have left, the daily budget, the cycle summary — runs from that day to the next one, not from the first of the month.
