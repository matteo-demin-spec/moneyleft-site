What MoneyLeft is

A personal finance app. You record what comes in and what goes out, and the app tells you how much you have left until your next payday. Your archive is kept on your device, and a backup copy is kept on your account, until you switch it off.

Account

To use MoneyLeft you need a MoneyLeft account, which is free: when it starts, the app asks you to create one or to sign in, and all you need is an email address and a password. If you are updating from a version without an account, you create the account or sign in, and the data you have on the phone becomes your account's data.

If you sign out of the account or the session expires, the app stops at the sign-in screen until you sign back in. From that screen you can export your data. You can delete the account at any time, from Settings › Account › Delete account: deleting it erases your data on the server and on the phone you delete it from, and only the records described in the privacy policy remain.

Keep your password to yourself and choose one you do not use anywhere else: anyone who knows it can get into your account and see your data, and you are responsible for what happens with your credentials, unless it is caused by a mistake on our part. If you think someone has found it out, reset it straight away from the sign-in screen and write to us.

You may not use MoneyLeft for illegal activities, nor try to access other people's data, get around the limits of the free plan or the subscription checks, query or overload the server with tools other than the app, extract data from it automatically, introduce viruses or malicious code, or copy, decompile and resell the app, except for what the law allows you to do anyway.

If you break these rules seriously, we may suspend or close your account. We first write to you at the account email to explain why and give you the chance to reply, unless we need to act at once to protect the service or other users. You can still export the data on your phone from the sign-in screen, and the data on the server is kept for 30 days before it is deleted: during that period you can ask us for a copy.

For this closure this notice applies, instead of the one provided for the closure of the service.

We do not ask you or push you to make any purchase: what to buy, how much and when is your decision. For this reason we are not liable for the choices you make on the basis of these numbers, nor for incorrect results caused by data entered wrongly or incompletely. Currencies and exchange rates

If you use more than one currency, MoneyLeft converts amounts with the reference rates published by the European Central Bank, the latest available. They are indicative rates: your bank or your card network apply their own rates and fees, and the right figure is the one on your statement. That is why you can always type in the amount actually charged, which takes precedence over the ECB rate. We are not responsible for differences between the indicative rate and the one applied by your bank.

Subscription

MoneyLeft can be used free of charge to record transactions, manage budgets, see the summary of the current cycle and month, and keep a backup copy on your account. The free plan has some limits on creating new items: up to 4 custom categories (subcategories are unlimited), up to 3 instalment plans open at the same time, up to 3 subscriptions. Reaching a limit does not affect what you have already created: it only prevents you from creating more until you move to Pro.

With the free plan, the built-in categories, the ones the app suggests to you at the start, cannot be renamed, recoloured, given a new icon, merged or deleted, and their built-in subcategories cannot be deleted: you can add subcategories of your own and remove them whenever you like. If you were already using MoneyLeft before version 1.1, this rule applies to you from the date stated in the notice the app shows you, and only once you have accepted these terms from that notice, as explained in "Changes to the service and to these terms"; any changes you had already made to the built-in categories stay as they are. For this change, the notice is the one the app shows you when you update.

The MoneyLeft Pro subscription removes these limits and adds: net worth forecasting beyond the first month, the 6-month projection beyond the first month, transaction analysis, saving suggestions, the spending recurrence profile, the summary beyond the current cycle or month, the comparison with the previous period, the freedom to rename, merge and delete all categories and subcategories, the use of multiple currencies for accounts and expenses, and syncing between your devices.

The subscription is recorded against your MoneyLeft account, not against the phone and not against the Apple Account: you find it again on any device you sign in to, even with a different Apple Account from the one you bought it with. If you sign out of the account the subscription remains yours: when you sign back in you find it again, and it does not have to be bought again.

The subscription is sold by Apple, not by us: the charge, the renewal, the refund and the cancellation always go through your Apple Account, from iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. We only record that the purchase is yours, and until when it is valid.

If you use the same phone with a different MoneyLeft account, the Pro features do not appear: they belong to whoever bought them, not to the device. And if you delete your account, the subscription remains active with Apple and continues to be charged until you cancel it: the record on our server, on the other hand, disappears, so you would no longer find it on another device. On the phone you bought it from, the Pro features come back even if you create a new account, with any email address: the app remembers that the deleted account was yours.

The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it expires. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Cancellation is handled by Apple: from iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from the "Manage subscription" button inside the app, which leads to the same place. Writing to us does not stop the renewal, because the charge does not go through us.

Promotional codes. If you receive a code, you can redeem it from Settings › MoneyLeft Pro › Redeem a code, or from the App Store. A code gives a free or discounted period: when that period ends the subscription continues at the full price of the chosen plan, which Apple shows you on the purchase screen before you confirm, and renews by itself, unless cancelled at least 24 hours before. Everything else in this section applies: cancellation is handled through Apple, and if the subscription ends your data stays where it is.

If the subscription ends, your data remains intact: only the Pro features listed above are switched off, and among them syncing between devices. The backup copy on your account carries on, unless you have switched it off, because it is not a Pro feature: what you write stays safe even without a subscription. Export and the backup you export yourself remain free.
